# What staff will do in Citius Connect

This guide explains the changes in the draft review. The team's business stages,
responsibilities and approval powers stay the same. The new internal post-tour
report is an additional task, described below. This draft has not been merged or
deployed to production.

All people, trips and amounts in the pictures are test examples. Captions distinguish
signed-in local screens from component examples. Each image opens at its original
size. A picture of a control does not prove its permissions or delivery behavior.

## Start work and find records

Open the same department or work list from the sidebar. Destinations still depend
on your role and permissions. On a phone, use the navigation button to open the
drawer. Search commands opens the existing command search; it does not search or
expose records outside your access.

The page title and main action sit above the work. Use the list search to narrow
the current page. Open Filters for the existing filter choices. When filters are
active, Clear filters is available beside the toolbar even with the panel closed.
It clears the current search, dates and list filters. It does not delete records.

On a desktop table, use Columns to choose visible columns and the horizontal
scroll controls to reach the rest. The Action column remains available. The main
row button names the next available action; the three-dot More menu holds other
permitted actions. Opening More does not submit, approve or delete anything. On a
phone, records use the compact list/card presentation.

### The number, the orange dot and unread updates mean different things

| Indicator | What it tells you | What clears or changes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gray number beside a sidebar destination | Work still needing attention under that destination's existing rules, within your role and assignment scope. It is not the total number of records. | The underlying work changes. Merely opening the page does not complete it. Destinations without an established actionable count do not receive an invented number. |
| Small orange dot beside that destination | Relevant new activity has arrived since you last successfully opened that destination. It is separate from the number. | Open the destination and let it load. This acknowledges the activity present for that visit. A later arrival restores the dot, including one that arrives during navigation. |
| Unread in Notifications or the bell | Personal updates you have not opened. | Open that specific update. Opening a work tab, the bell, or the All filter does not mark every update read. |

For example, a destination can show two work items and an orange dot. After you
open it successfully, the dot can disappear while the number remains two. Your
Inbox can still contain unread updates. None of those actions completes the two
items. Hovering over a link or a failed navigation does not acknowledge the new
activity. These indicators follow the signed-in person, including after refresh
or switching staff accounts. If a count is incomplete, it says at least that
number, or shows an ellipsis when it cannot establish a positive count. It does
not claim there is no work. Activity records an arrival, so a dot can remain
until you visit even if the work has since closed or moved to another owner.

## Read an update and open the work

1. Open the bell for a quick view, or choose Notifications for the full personal inbox.
2. Use Unread for updates you have not opened. Use All to include read history.
3. Open the update itself. It marks that update read and takes you to its linked
   Query, Job Card or other authorized work. An assignment opens the work, rather
   than asking a SPOC to use a head-only assignment screen.
4. Continue the task through its usual form or action. Reading a notice is not
   confirmation, approval or completion of that task.

A later update stays unread even if you were already viewing the inbox. When you
have read everything, the inbox offers the read updates. With no history, it says
there are no notifications yet. A loading message means the updates have not
loaded; it does not mean there are none. A failed read shows an error and keeps
the update unread so you can retry.

## Sales and Contracting

The sequence remains Query, assigned preparation, Proposal, Sales decision,
confirmed offer, then Job Card. This redesign does not remove required information,
change prices or taxes, replace supporting documents, or create a new approval step.

### Create or continue a Query

Open All Sales Queries. Choose New Query to enter a new enquiry, or find the
existing row and use its available action. Check the client, destination, dates,
guest count, budget and contact details before explicitly saving. Search and filters
only change what you see in the list.

Use the existing handoff action when the Query is ready for Contracting. The
selected Contracting or Ticketing SPOC receives their assignment. Contracting and
Operations heads receive intake oversight. Directors and Admin receive the intake
copy where that event calls for Management. Head of Ticketing receives the intake
when Ticketing is required. Other Contracting SPOCs do not receive a team-wide
assignment message.

### Prepare or revise a Proposal

The assigned preparer opens the work from the list or assignment update. Complete
the existing costing and itinerary information, then use the explicit save and
handoff actions. The Proposal form groups related fields and keeps its actions
reachable while its content scrolls. A phone does not remove the itinerary or
costing fields to make the form look shorter.

When a Proposal is ready, the assigned Sales SPOC receives the handoff. Sales Head
is the fallback when one Sales owner cannot be identified. A date or destination
revision goes to the assigned Contracting SPOC, the required Ticketing SPOC and
relevant heads. Ticketing marked Not required does not become a new Ticketing task.
An order-lost notice follows the assigned Contracting owner and head, plus the
assigned Ticketing owner when required.

### What happens when a save is pending or fails

After you press Save, the form pauses its controls while that request is pending.
Wait for its result rather than pressing Save again or changing values halfway
through the request. If it fails, the draft remains and editing becomes available
for correction and retry. Required fields and validation remain. A save is never
triggered just because a field was prefilled or a dropdown was opened.

Do not treat a draft in the loaded page as a saved record. Use the explicit save
before closing or reloading the browser. Navigation and draft protection differ
by form; the specific Account and internal-report behavior is described below.

## Heads assigning work or doing it themselves

Open the existing team-assignment action for the Query or Job Card. Choose the
responsible SPOC in the relevant department and save the allocation. Heads can
select themselves for their own department's work. For example, a Head of
Ticketing can take the Ticketing assignment and prepare its costing. This does
not grant a head another department's role or bypass record scope.

The selected owner receives the assignment. A direct Job Card assignment opens
that Job Card. Reallocation changes who is responsible for future work and the
recipient checks for delayed notices. An old queued email does not simply transfer
to a newly assigned person. The new person uses their own current assignment and
permitted record links.

For ticket attention and scheduled reminders, the relevant heads retain oversight
when a SPOC is unassigned or inactive. The system does not substitute the entire
department. A person who is both a head and an assigned owner receives one copy,
rather than duplicate copies for each role.

## Operations, Ticketing and Tour Managers

### Continue from the Job Card

Open Job Cards or follow an authorized update to the relevant trip. Check the
client, destination and dates before using its operational actions. The Job Card
continues to show tour context, documents, readiness and checklist work. Its
presentation does not redefine completion or remove a blocker.

When a valid Job Card is already selected, supported new operational forms can
start with that selection. An explicit record link or an existing record being
edited takes precedence. Check the selection and fill the remaining required
information, then save explicitly. This convenience does not create a booking,
assign a traveller or submit a record on its own. Not every form has the same
prefill; Visa shares the form presentation but is not one of these prefill kinds.

Traveller, hotel, PNR, ticket, seat and expense work retains its existing fields,
document handling, calculations and permissions. The [complete screen inventory](screenshot-walkthrough.md#picture-and-state-inventory)
links the distinct desktop and phone forms. Long forms have an internal scroll
area and reachable actions; a viewport picture is not a claim that all fields
are visible at once.

### Respond to Ticketing attention

Name-change and reissue attention goes to the assigned Operations and Ticketing
SPOCs, Operations Head and Head of Ticketing. Open the linked ticket and continue
the existing correction workflow. Reading the alert does not change the ticket
or send a new message. Unassigned work remains with the relevant heads for
oversight. Unrelated Operations and Ticketing staff and extra email-alert role
subscriptions do not expand this audience.

### Travel and Tour Manager work

Staff travel keeps the person, dates, destination, optional Job Card and explicit
save. If a selected person has not finished loading, wait for that result rather
than guessing an identity. A failed save retains the entered travel draft.

The selected staff Tour Manager receives their allocation with the trip and
reporting context. Other Tour Managers do not receive that assignment notice.
External Tour Manager invitations and profile history keep their existing rules.
The [travel and manager pictures](crm-redesign.md#staff-journey-coverage)
show the shared presentation; they do not add new invitation or approval powers.

## Prepare the internal tour report

This is the new internal report requested for the Tour Manager or Operations
Manager after a trip. It is separate from traveller feedback and older team notes.
Guest request links, consent and photos remain in their own existing workflow.

### Open the report for the correct trip

From the Job Card, open Feedback beside the JC number, then Internal tour report.
The report stays inside Citius Connect. Its Job Card selector lets you open that
trip's existing report or begin its new report. Check the JC reference before
entering answers.

The assigned Tour Manager, assigned Operations owner and Operations Head can
prepare within their existing record and Cement scope. Admin and Directors do
not gain preparation rights merely from those roles. If you can view a submitted
report but are not an authorized preparer, it is read-only.

**Open Feedback next to the JC number.** This complete signed-in local frame uses
test data and shows the internal report alongside the retained feedback choices.

[![Job Card Feedback dropdown](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/74224976-05cb-41c5-8e5e-ced3c093f2a6)](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/74224976-05cb-41c5-8e5e-ced3c093f2a6)

### Complete all six sections

| Section | What the preparer does |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Tour details | Check the client/company, destination, tour dates, number of guests and Tour Manager. Choose the programme type. Reliable JC information starts the form, but the report keeps its own editable snapshot. Saving it does not rewrite the Job Card. |
| 2. Overall tour performance | Give five separate ratings from 1, Poor, to 5, Excellent, for the tour, client satisfaction, itinerary, hotel/food/transport and handling unexpected situations. Record the three biggest highlights. |
| 3. Hit & Miss report | Select all relevant Hits and Misses. If there is a Miss, explain the main issue, how it was resolved and what should change next time. An empty list means no items to report, not an automatically selected example. |
| 4. Supplier performance | Rate Hotel, Restaurant/catering, Transport, DMC/local operator, Event venue/production and Airline from 1 to 5, or choose N.A. N.A. is a distinct choice, not zero. A score of 1 or 2 opens the question about what went wrong and whether you would recommend that supplier again. Good or N.A. ratings do not require that extra comment. |
| 5. Financial and operational closure | Answer the additional-cost, budget-variation, refund/recovery and billing-closure choices. Where additional or pending work needs follow-up, enter the amount, reason and responsible person. This records closure information; it does not approve a payment or change a calculation. |
| 6. Final assessment and ownership | Give the separate overall score from 1 to 10, choose Excellent/Good/Needs Improvement, choose whether to repeat the destination and itinerary, and record the top three learnings. Citius records the authenticated preparer's name and submission date. You do not enter another person's identity. |

### Save a draft or submit the finished report

Use Save draft when you need to return to it. A draft remains limited to the
current authorized preparers and Admin. It sends no submission alert. Within the
loaded app, unsaved entries return when you switch feedback tabs or Job Cards.
Save the draft before closing or reloading the browser.

Use Submit report when the answers are ready to share. The form checks required
answers, conditional details and rating ranges. Pending controls prevent repeated
submission. A failed save retains the draft for retry. If another session changed
the report, Citius rejects a stale save rather than overwriting that change.

Submission makes a read-only snapshot available to active CRM staff. It sends one
submission event to active Directors, Finance Heads and the Sales/Contracting
people actually related to that Job Card. Admin is not automatically copied. The
whole Finance team, unrelated heads and generic extra-alert subscriptions are not
added. Overlapping roles receive one copy. Email carries the JC reference and
report link, not the report answers, financial details or private free text.

## Read or archive a submitted report

All active, provisioned CRM staff can read the submitted report snapshot. This
does not grant access to the full Job Card, customer record or unrelated trip
documents. Drafts do not become visible to everyone. Customers and public visitors
cannot read internal reports.

Open the submitted report to read its six sections and recorded ownership. It is
read-only. The [complete submitted desktop report](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/113f07dc-4bb0-42fb-ab8f-e84fa2379cc9)
and [complete submitted phone report](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fef40b7f-f24f-456a-bb7b-185c1e66fd58)
show the actual local test result.

Only Admin can use Archive report on the new structured report. The confirmation
explains that it hides the report and stops pending submission alerts. Confirm
only when that is intended; Cancel leaves the report in place. Archive retains
the snapshot and audit trail. It is recoverable, not permanent deletion.

Admin can open Archived reports and choose Restore report. Restore sends no new
submission alert and cannot replace a newer current report. A Directors-only
account does not receive the Admin archive action. Older team-note moderation
rules remain separate.

## Finance, HR and Management

Finance staff continue the existing expense, proof, approval and reimbursement
steps. Managers, Finance and Directors keep the approval rights they already had.
The compact tables and forms change presentation and reduce repeated selection;
they do not authorize a new approver or change financial outputs.

Invoice-overdue reminders use Accounts/Finance heads because an invoice has no
assigned Finance SPOC field. They do not guess an owner or email all Finance
staff. Other scheduled workflow reminders follow the current assigned SPOCs and
relevant heads. Disabled notification controls and inactive staff remain excluded.

HR and staff continue the current leave request and approval process. The
configured head approver, any distinct final authority, HR review and requester
notices stay at their existing stages. This PR does not change leave authority,
routing or add a Management CC based on a reporting chart.

Management means active Directors plus Admin only for events that call for
Management. It is not a blanket copy on private material or every event. The new
internal report has its own narrower, named submission audience described above.

For the exact event-by-event audiences, use [R01 to R15](README.md#recipient-rules).
[D01 to D03](README.md#confirmed-decisions) record the business decisions already
confirmed by the user. They are not unanswered review questions.

## Use the email link

Workflow emails now use the Citius logo, a short event heading, useful record
details and one primary action. Open that action to continue in Citius. Sign-in
and record permissions still apply. An email link does not approve, charge,
submit or complete the work for you. A descriptive secondary link provides the
same destination; the plain-text version retains the complete link.

Staff do not choose how to resend a workflow update. Temporary send failures
retry through the existing background process. Before a delayed send or retry,
Citius checks current assignment, access and whether the message still has a
purpose. An inactive person or an obsolete queued assignment is not a reason to
email the entire team.

Authentication emails keep their existing verification/reset actions, subjects
and token meaning. Contact enquiries keep Reply available to reach the sender.
Email does not include passport information, private report answers or financial
free text merely because those fields exist in the record.

## Account and public-site changes

These remain visually separate from Citius Connect. They are usability changes,
not a CRM-style redesign of the public site or Customer Account.

In Account, choose Edit from the displayed profile. The draft starts with the
current values. Moving between Account tabs keeps it without submitting it. Save
is explicit; pending controls prevent competing edits, and a failure keeps the
draft. Cancel does not save. Email and passport read-only rules remain. Save
before closing or reloading the browser.

**Account edits survive tab changes.** This full signed-in local phone frame shows
a retained synthetic profile draft.

[![Retained Account draft](actual/customer-profile-draft-phone.png)](actual/customer-profile-draft-phone.png)

On Contact, phone users reach the form before the office maps. The existing fields,
consent and explicit send remain. While sending, fields pause so a later edit
cannot be lost when the earlier request completes. A failed send retains the
values for retry. No automatic enquiry or new acknowledgement email was added.

Services retains the circular selector. All choices fit narrow screens and can
be reached from the keyboard. The selected description appears below the circle
on a phone. The [public and Account review](site-usability.md) contains the full
before/after pictures and pending-state views for U01 to U05.

## Director review questions

- Can staff identify the next action on a populated Query, Proposal and Job Card
  without explanation? Name the screen and the unclear label if a change is needed.
- Are the internal report questions clear for a Tour Manager completing them on
  a phone? Point to the section or field that needs different wording.
- Do the assignment, security and Contact emails give enough context before the
  main action? Identify any line that should change.
- Is an important task missing from this guide or its picture inventory? Name the
  role, starting screen and intended result so it can be reviewed precisely.

These are usability and wording review questions. Management membership, head
self-assignment, leave approval, targeted reminders and the internal-report
audience remain the confirmed rules. Detailed tests, exact revisions, local-only
limits and the unresolved native Notes error remain in the PR's collapsed report
and linked verification documents.
