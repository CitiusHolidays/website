# Event Photo Booth

The Event Photo Booth is a planned Citius Holidays experience for event participants to create
and share Destination Photos or Framed Photos. It is separate from Sacred Bharat Social Editions, Customer Travel
Accounts and staff CRM operations.

## Language

**Selfie Point**:
The physical event installation where people pose for photographs. Its QR code leads to the Event
Photo Booth, which also accepts photos taken elsewhere.
_Avoid_: Treating the physical installation and the digital experience as the same thing

**Event Photo Booth**:
The event-limited website experience that combines a participant's photo with a predefined
Destination Scene to make a shareable Destination Photo.
_Avoid_: WhatsApp bot, Sacred Bharat Social Edition, permanent public photo editor

**Event Participant**:
A visitor using the Event Photo Booth to create and share a Destination Photo. Participation
requires neither a customer account nor a travel enquiry.
_Avoid_: CRM Client, Yatri, account holder, treating participation as marketing consent

**Event Manager**:
An Admin, a Director, or a specifically assigned staff member authorized to manage the Event Photo
Booth, its scenes and aggregate usage. This authority does not grant access to participant photos.
_Avoid_: New CRM job role, every staff member, photo moderator, permission to change unrelated staff access

**Event Staff Assignment**:
An explicit grant from an Admin or Director allowing an existing staff member to act as an Event
Manager. Assigned staff cannot grant or revoke assignments or change wider Staff Workspace permissions.
_Avoid_: New staff job role, self-assignment, granting event management to a public participant

**Destination Scene**:
A predefined place setting selected for a Destination Photo. Scenes are grouped under Travel and
Pilgrimage so participants can choose the kind of destination they want.
_Avoid_: Itinerary, booked journey, user-generated destination

**Source Photo**:
The participant-supplied photograph containing the people to preserve in the Destination Photo.
It may show one person, a couple or a small family.
_Avoid_: Identity document, profile photo, consent to retain or republish the photograph

**Destination Photo**:
The shareable composition of the people from a Source Photo and a chosen Destination Scene,
preserving their faces and clothing while replacing the original background.
_Avoid_: Proof of a visit, face swap, generated likeness, Citius-owned marketing photograph

**Framed Photo**:
An explicitly chosen alternative that preserves the whole Source Photo, including its original
background, inside a branded frame when a participant cannot obtain a satisfactory cutout.
_Avoid_: Successful background removal, silently substituting the fallback for a Destination Photo

**Event Availability**:
The manually controlled Open or Closed state of the Event Photo Booth. Open admits anyone with
the event link; Closed presents visitors with a useful closed message and travel links instead of
new photo creation. Authenticated Admins and Directors retain participant access in either state.
_Avoid_: Scheduled start/end times, attendee verification, invalidating photos already downloaded
