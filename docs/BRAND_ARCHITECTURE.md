# Citius brand architecture

This page is the parent/sub-brand boundary for public copy, navigation, metadata, and design
reviews. It is intentionally descriptive: it does not introduce a second runtime theme or require
new logos before the brand team approves assets.

## Relationship

| Identity | Role | Audience | Relationship to the parent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Citius Holidays** | Parent travel company and service promise | Leisure travellers, corporate buyers, partners | Owns the company name, service relationship, legal/footer language, and primary travel CTA |
| **Citius Connect** | Staff operations product | Sales, Contracting, Operations, Ticketing, Accounts, HR | An endorsed product of Citius Holidays; use the product name only in authenticated staff and product contexts |
| **Sacred Bharat** | Consumer spiritual-travel and gamification experience | Pilgrimage travellers and returning guests | An endorsed Citius Holidays experience with its own editorial voice; never present it as a separate legal company |

## Naming and placement rules

1. Use **Citius Holidays** when a visitor needs to understand who provides the travel service,
   sends a confirmation, owns a policy, or receives a contact request.
2. Use **Citius Connect** for the staff CRM, its sign-in, internal workflows, and operational help
   copy. Do not use Connect as the public booking brand.
3. Use **Sacred Bharat** for `/sacred-bharat`, the Soul Score, temple check-ins, trails, badges, and
   Journey Planner. Keep a restrained “by Citius Holidays” endorsement in the footer or about copy
   when the legal/company relationship matters.
4. Do not merge Sacred Bharat points, badges, or local-progress language into the Citius Connect
   CRM vocabulary. Sacred Bharat progress is a consumer engagement layer, not a CRM record.
5. Keep transactional/authentication sender names and support details under Citius Holidays until a
   separate legal sender is approved.

## Shared and distinct visual language

- **Shared:** care, precision, Indian hospitality, purposeful journeys, accessible controls, and a
  warm human service tone.
- **Citius Holidays:** navy/blue authority, warm paper surfaces, restrained orange/lime accents,
  editorial travel imagery, and a clear service CTA.
- **Citius Connect:** operational density, high-contrast data hierarchy, and compact interaction
  feedback. It may use the parent colors but is not required to look like the marketing site.
- **Sacred Bharat:** contemplative dark navy, warm saffron/gold, cream, and spacious editorial
  pacing. Its spiritual imagery and symbols must not be used to imply a religious guarantee or
  exclusive ownership of a tradition.

The public token foundation lives in [`docs/PUBLIC_VISUAL_IDENTITY.md`](PUBLIC_VISUAL_IDENTITY.md).
Operational/account palettes are scoped separately so a brand experiment cannot silently change
staff workflows or customer account data surfaces.

## Metadata and content checklist

- Page titles and descriptions identify the actual experience (`Citius Holidays`, `Citius Connect`,
  or `Sacred Bharat`) rather than combining three names into one title.
- Open Graph images use the owning experience's approved image treatment and a stable absolute URL.
- Public support, privacy, and terms links remain attributable to Citius Holidays.
- Sacred Bharat claims describe the product experience (check-ins, trails, badges, planning), not a
  promise of spiritual outcomes.
- Product UI and email copy use the same capitalization: **Citius Connect** and **Sacred Bharat**.

## Approval boundary

This architecture is the baseline for public marketing and product reviews. A new endorsed identity,
logo lockup, color, font, or legal sender requires brand-owner approval and a focused design review;
do not infer approval from a generated mockup or an inspiration gallery.
