# Keep participant photos outside staff event management

The [Event Photo Booth](../event-photo-booth/CONTEXT.md) gives staff control over the event
and its scenes, plus aggregate usage counts, while participant photos remain outside staff and CRM
records. Participants save or share their results during the current session; Citius provides no
photo archive, personal result links, staff photo gallery or implicit marketing-reuse permission.
This prioritizes a quick visitor experience and limited handling of personal photos over later
retrieval, so Citius cannot recover a result that the participant did not save.

Photo processing is browser-first. Temporary hosted processing may be considered only if testing
demonstrates a need and the provider's quality, cost and deletion policy have been verified; no
provider is selected by this decision. A later travel enquiry is a separate, explicit participant
action and does not include the participant's photo.
