# Citius Journal: director's publishing guide

The blog is now the **Citius Journal**: a magazine for both pilgrims and corporate and MICE
clients. It is designed to look complete with only a few stories, so publishing rarely is
fine. One well-written story every month or two is better than frequent short ones.

The website menu still says **Blog**; the page itself is titled Citius Journal.

## Before you start (development team)

The development team publishes the updated Sanity Studio and the new website. Until then the
new fields below won't appear. They will tell you when it is ready.

## Writing a story in Sanity Studio

Open the Studio, choose **Post**, and fill in:

1. **Title.** Plain and specific, for example "Why the Next Two Quarters Demand Smarter MICE
   Planning".
2. **Slug.** Click Generate.
3. **Author.** Choose yourself. In your **Author** record, fill in **Role** (for example
   "Director") and **Office** once; they appear under every story you write.
4. **Main image.** Upload the cover, then:
   - set the focus point (the hotspot) on the most important part of the picture,
   - fill in **Alternative text**: one sentence describing the picture for people who can't see it
     (for example "Aerial view of a palm-lined beach beside turquoise water").
   - Avoid covers with the title written on the picture. The page already shows the title in
     large type, so text on the image appears twice and may be cut off on phones.
5. **Excerpt.** One or two sentences summarising the story. It is shown on the Journal page
   and in Google results.
6. **Categories.** Pick what fits (for example Pilgrimage, MICE, Group travel).
7. **Related Spiritual Trail** (optional). If the story is about a pilgrimage trail on our site, choose
   it. A "The route in this story" link to that trail then appears at the end of the story.
8. **Published at.** Set the date. Every story needs one.
9. **Body.** Write in short paragraphs with clear headings. For each picture inside the
   story, fill in its **Alternative text** and, if useful, a **Caption**.

Then **Publish**. The site updates within a few minutes.

## Fix the existing stories (one-off, about 20 minutes)

- **"Tourism in the Age of Disruption"** has no publish date. Open it, set **Published at**,
  and publish.
- Open each **Author** record and add **Role** and **Office**.
- Open each story and add **Alternative text** to its main image, and an **Excerpt**.

## How to check it

After publishing, open the Journal on a computer and on a phone:

- The story appears at the top of the Journal page with its date and reading time.
- The title, author, role and date show immediately, without waiting for anything to fade in.
- The cover picture is not cropped badly and nothing is written over it.
- At the end of the story there is a "Talk to the team" section, and "The route in this
  story" if you chose a related trail.

Send the development team a screenshot of anything that looks wrong.
