## Cultural guardrails applied

- Recognition stays focused on place, riverfront, and architecture; the edition does not score
  devotion, knowledge of ritual, or spirituality.
- No deity, sacred icon, funeral rite, or active worshipper is used as a joke, reward, or wrong-answer
  treatment.
- Reveal copy distinguishes documented architectural facts from tradition and avoids claims of
  spiritual outcomes.
- The legacy tracker records and schema remain intact. Public legacy routes now lead to Edition 001,
  but this release does not delete or migrate any prior yatri data.
